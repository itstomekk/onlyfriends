A feed with only the people you know

  • Friends, nothing else

    A post is kept only if its author is on your friends list. Facebook friends, Instagram mutual follows. Everything else is dropped before you see it.

  • Read-only, your own login

    It reads the feed in a browser session you logged into yourself. It never likes, comments, follows or posts, and never asks for your password.

  • Local, private, yours

    The page runs on 127.0.0.1. Your friends list and saved photos stay on your computer. There is no hosted version and no account.

Try it in three commands

Needs Python 3.9 or newer. The demo uses made-up people only, so you can look around without connecting anything.

git clone https://github.com/itstomekk/onlyfriends.git
cd onlyfriends
python serve.py --demo

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8766/.

Then use your own feed

Install the browser driver, add your profile names, log in once in a visible window, refresh.

npm i -g agent-browser
cp config.example.json config.json
python refresh.py
python serve.py

Full steps are in the README.

Plain-words limits

  • Unofficial. Not affiliated with Meta, Facebook or Instagram. When they change their pages, it can break.
  • Check the platform terms. Automated reading may be against them. Your choice, your risk. The defaults are slow and gentle on purpose.
  • Local only. Nothing is uploaded. No hosted version, now or later.
  • Demo data. Every name, post and picture on this page is fictional.
